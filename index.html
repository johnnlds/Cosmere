This is a curated, expandable character dataset, not a verified exhaustive index of every person mentioned in the books. Descriptions and relationships contain spoilers for all three novels. Relationship labels summarize important story connections rather than every interaction. Historical figures, supernatural beings, and briefly mentioned characters are included when relevant to the trilogy.

Research starting points: The Final Empire, The Well of Ascension, and The Hero of Ages on the Coppermind. This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Brandon Sanderson.
