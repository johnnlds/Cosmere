How to read these dates

FE means Final Empire dating on Scadrial. R means Rosharan dating. These are separate local calendars; do not compare 1025 FE and 1173 R as if they were years on one shared calendar. A Rosharan year has 500 days, while the Scadrian calendar is different.

Confirmed means a published source supports the year or year range. Estimated means the year is reconstructed from relative statements. Relative means only the order is dependable. Conflicting means sources or reconstructions do not agree, so the timeline preserves the uncertainty.

This is a first-pass chronology of major milestones, not a claim to reproduce Brandon Sanderson's private Master Cosmere Timeline. The author has said publicly that some cross-series intervals remain uncanonized and subject to revision.
